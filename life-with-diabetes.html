Life with Diabetes

Living with diabetes brings unique challenges, but millions of people thrive every day. We're here to support you at every step of your journey.

Newly Diagnosed?

A diabetes diagnosis can feel overwhelming. Here's what to know first.

🩺 Talk to Your Care Team

Your first step is building a strong diabetes care team — your doctor, diabetes educator, dietitian, and pharmacist. They'll help you create a personalized management plan.

📊 Understand Your Numbers

Learn your A1C, blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood glucose targets. The ADA recommends an A1C below 7% for most adults with diabetes.

🥗 Nutrition Basics

No single "diabetes diet" exists, but focusing on whole foods, limiting refined carbs and sugars, and eating consistent meals will help stabilize blood sugar.

💪 Get Moving

Physical activity is one of the most powerful tools for managing diabetes. Even 30 minutes of walking most days can significantly improve blood sugar control.
